On the Apple Watch and the iPhone it provides a match clock, the recording of goals, cards, time penalties, substitutions and voice notes, the creation of special reports, match reports and summaries, a fill-in helper for the associations’ systems, expense overviews, a running analysis, a rules quiz, an observer mode and the import of appointments and line-ups.
