Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · applies to the “Whistlebook” apps for iPhone and Apple Watch

In short: Whistlebook stores your matches, events, notes, reports, voice notes and expenses on your iPhone and your Apple Watch. There is no user account, no server of my own, no advertising, no tracking and no usage statistics. Text recognition, speech recognition and the optional wording assistant run on the device. If you use iCloud, Apple syncs your data to your private iCloud — I have no access to it. The only things that leave your device are purchase information for managing Whistlebook Pro and whatever you share yourself.

2. What is processed and why

2.1 Your match data (on your devices only)

Everything you create in the app — matches and appointments, teams, line-ups, goals, cards, time penalties, substitutions, match times, notes, special reports, expenses including signature, observations, quiz results and settings — is stored solely in the app container of your iPhone and your Apple Watch. Between your Apple Watch and your iPhone this data is transferred directly via Apple's WatchConnectivity, without a server in between. This data is not transmitted to me. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract of use).

2.2 Data of other persons that you enter

As a referee you also enter information about other persons, such as names and shirt numbers of players, roles of officials, verbatim statements in a special report or — in observer mode — notes on an observed referee. Like all match data, this information stays on your devices; I have no access to it. You are yourself responsible for its use: use it only for your role as a referee or observer and in accordance with your association’s requirements, and delete it when you no longer need it.

2.3 iCloud sync (optional, under your control)

If iCloud is enabled for Whistlebook and you have switched on sync in the app, your match data is synced through Apple's CloudKit into your private iCloud database so that it is available across your devices and survives a device change. Apple is responsible for the storage as part of your Apple account; I have no access to this data and can neither view nor analyse it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You can turn sync off at any time in the app or in the iOS Settings.

2.4 Apple Health, workouts and running route (Apple Watch)

During a match, the Apple Watch starts a football workout at your request. With your permission in Apple Health, the app reads heart rate, distance and active energy and saves the workout, including the running route, in Apple Health. For the route, the Watch uses your precise location (GPS) while the workout is running; no location is recorded outside a match. On the iPhone, the app reads your workout and your heart rate from Apple Health in order to calculate the running analysis (distance, sprints, heat map, zones). This calculation takes place on the device; health and location data are not transmitted to me and are not used for advertising. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR (your consent, given by granting permission in iOS). You can revoke the permissions at any time in the Health app or in the iOS Settings.

2.5 Microphone, voice notes and speech recognition

You can record voice notes during and after the match. The recordings are stored locally and are converted into text with Apple's speech recognition on the device; for this, iOS downloads a language pack from Apple once if needed. The recordings themselves are not transmitted to me or to Apple. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; you grant access to the microphone through iOS and can revoke it there at any time.

2.6 Calendar

If you allow it, the app reads events from your calendar in order to suggest matches from them (e.g. appointments that your association writes into your calendar). The app writes nothing to your calendar. Only what you confirm is adopted. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent, given by granting permission in iOS).

2.7 Import from emails, photos, PDFs and the clipboard

You can paste appointments and line-ups or adopt them from a photo, screenshot or PDF. The app accesses only the image or document you select and reads the text with Apple's text recognition on the device. If the built-in rules leave gaps and your device supports Apple Intelligence, Apple's language model additionally reads the text on the device; only values that appear verbatim in the text are adopted. On request, the same model can help you word a report; verbatim quotes are not passed to the model in the process. None of this leaves your device. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

2.8 Notifications

Reminders, for example of the deadline for a match report, are local notifications that your device schedules itself. There is no push server and no transmission to me.

2.9 Purchases and subscriptions

“Whistlebook Pro” is purchased through the Apple App Store. Apple handles payment and billing; I receive no payment data from Apple, only aggregated, non-personal sales statistics.

To verify and manage the purchase status I use RevenueCat as a processor. This involves a random app user identifier assigned by RevenueCat, the purchase receipt issued by Apple and the technical details required for the request (app version, operating system, App Store country, IP address at the time of the request). No email addresses, names, match data or payment details are transmitted to RevenueCat. The purpose is to unlock the correct features and to let you restore purchases. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Which matches count as your three trial matches is remembered by the app locally on your device.

2.10 Export and sharing

Reports, summaries, expense overviews and exports (PDF, CSV) are created by the app on the device. Where you send them — for example by email, into your association’s system or to a club — is your decision via the iOS share sheet; the terms of the respective recipient apply. Whistlebook itself does not submit anything to an association.

3. No advertising, no tracking, no usage statistics

Whistlebook contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no social media plugins and no cross-device tracking. No advertising identifier (IDFA) is used and no App Tracking Transparency dialog is shown, because no tracking takes place. The app does not collect anonymous usage counts either.

5. Retention

Your match data remains stored until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the local data; data held in iCloud is deleted through the iOS Settings (Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage). You manage workouts in Apple Health in the Health app. Purchase information held by RevenueCat is retained for the duration of the subscription or purchase and beyond as required by commercial and tax law.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). You may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Since I do not store any personal data about you myself, your match data resides solely with you; under “Settings → Data → Export all data” you can create a complete copy yourself at any time.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

7. Children and young people

Whistlebook is aimed at referees and may also be used by young referees. Since there is no account and no data is transmitted to me, the provider does not process any data of minors. Purchases are made through Apple; the Family Sharing settings apply there, such as “Ask to Buy”.
